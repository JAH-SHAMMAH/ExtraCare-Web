What farmers need from technology

Farmers adopt tools that are cheap, simple and pay back within a season. We build around that.

From anywhere, in one click

See every field, pond and poultry house on your phone or laptop, wherever you are, and record a day's work in a few taps.

Works without good network

Records save on the phone and send when there is signal. Alerts can go by SMS, so farmers without smartphones are included.

In any language

Screens and messages in the language you and your workers speak, with big buttons and few words.

Records that show profit

Every naira spent on seed, feed, labour and medicine against what you sold, so you see what each field, pond or house earns.

Fewer losses after harvest

Stock and storage reminders so produce, fish and eggs are sold or stored well before they spoil.

Better ways to sell

Orders on WhatsApp, an online shop or a buyer list, with payment by transfer and a record of who owes.

For cooperatives, aggregators and programmes

Cooperatives and aggregators

Member lists, inputs given on credit, produce collected from each member, repayments and payouts in one system.

Outgrower schemes

Track the seed, fertiliser and feed you give farmers, what they deliver back and what each one is owed.

Programmes and NGOs

Farmer registration, messages to farmers and progress reports that work in the field, offline first.
