Pond and batch records

Stocking date, number and size of fingerlings, and the history of every pond and tank in one view.

Water quality log

Oxygen, pH, ammonia and temperature from your test kit, with an alert when a reading is outside the safe range.

Feeding and feed cost

Feed given per pond each day, what it cost, and how much feed it takes to grow each kilo of fish.
